# Endoscopy Datasets

This page lists datasets used for training and evaluating multimodal models in **endoscopy and surgical vision**, including video–text datasets and visual question answering datasets.

---

## Endoscopy Multimodal Datasets

| Dataset | Modalities | Images | Text | Paper | Date | Tasks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [MedStreamBench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Venn2024/MedStreamBench) | Video, Text | 22 source datasets | 5,419 QA pairs (paper total) | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01751) | 2026-07-02 | streaming video understanding |
| EndoBench | Img, Text | 21 source datasets | 6,832 VQA pairs | [NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/02ff2906a49d985808e7ba8798b9f9cd-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html); [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23601) | 2025-05-29 | perception, localization, clinical reasoning |
| EndoVis-18-VQLA | Video, Text | 2k | 12k | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.11692); [ICRA](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10160403) | 2023-06-20 | VQA |

---

Counts are paper-reported; source images/videos may require separate access.
